# Advanced Imputation Techniques

## Model-Based Methods

- **K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN)** - Use values from similar observations
- **MICE (Multiple Imputation by Chained Equations)** - Iterative modeling approach
- **Matrix Factorization** - Low-rank approximation methods
- **Deep Learning** - Neural networks for complex patterns

## Advantages
✅ Preserve data relationships  
✅ Account for uncertainty  
✅ Better performance on complex datasets
