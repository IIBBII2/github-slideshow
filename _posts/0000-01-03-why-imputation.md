# Why Imputation Matters

Missing data can cause problems:

- 🚫 **Reduced sample size** - Many algorithms can't handle missing values
- 📊 **Biased results** - Systematic missing data can skew analysis
- 💥 **Model failures** - Most ML models require complete data
- 🔍 **Loss of information** - Dropping rows wastes valuable data
