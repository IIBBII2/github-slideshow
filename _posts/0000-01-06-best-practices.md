# Best Practices

## Guidelines for Choosing Methods

1. 📊 **Understand your data** - Analyze missing data patterns
2. 🎯 **Match method to data type** - Categorical vs. numerical
3. ⚖️ **Consider the amount of missingness** - Simple vs. complex methods
4. 🔬 **Evaluate impact** - Compare models with different imputation strategies
5. 📝 **Document decisions** - Track what imputation methods were used

## Remember
Missing data is not always random - investigate why data is missing!
